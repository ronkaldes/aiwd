# Brand cheat-sheet template (for Step 6 — the deck)

Fill this in for **your company** (5 minutes), then paste the relevant parts into Claude so your management deck looks and sounds unmistakably like yours. If your company already has a **brand skill installed in Claude**, skip this file — just say "use the [skill name] skill" and it does it properly.

> Don't know your brand values? Ask whoever owns marketing/design for the "brand guidelines" — or just pick the colors and font from your company website. Close enough beats blank.

### Worked example (so you can see the shape)

> Make this deck on-brand for AcmeCo: charcoal `#1f2129` dominant background, signal orange
> `#ff6b35` as the main accent, warm gray `#f4f2ee` for panels, Inter font, **sentence case**
> everywhere, one idea per slide. Spell the company `AcmeCo` and the product `Acme Cloud`.
> Put a thin orange underline beneath one key word per headline. Keep the tone confident and
> plainspoken, lead with outcomes then numbers, and close on the line "Ship it simple."
